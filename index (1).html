Tips and keyboard shortcuts
Tap a bar on the sheet, choose a note length, then tap a spot on the fretboard to write that note. On a keyboard: type a fret number (frets above 12 too), ↑ ↓ change string, x mutes, space adds a rest, ← → move, Backspace deletes, w h q e s set the length, “.” makes it dotted, t ties. Press any key or tap the sheet to stop playback. The ⌄ button hides the fretboard to give you more room.
